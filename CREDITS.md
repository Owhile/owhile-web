# Credits

## Hero artwork

**Lesser Ury**, *Bahnhof Nollendorfplatz bei Nacht* (1925). Märkisches Museum, Berlin.
Photograph by Wikimedia contributor *anagoria*.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1925_Ury_Bahnhof_Nollendorfplatz_bei_Nacht_anagoria.JPG
- Status: **Public domain.** Ury died in 1931, so the painting is out of copyright
  (`PD-old-80-expired`); the photograph is tagged `PD-Art`, Wikimedia's position that a faithful
  reproduction of a public-domain 2D work generates no new copyright. Verified via the Commons API:
  `LicenseShortName: Public domain`, `AttributionRequired: false`, `Restrictions: (empty)`.
- Used cropped, resized and colour-graded, which the public domain permits.

**Attribution is not required.** It is given anyway: some jurisdictions — Germany in particular, after
the 2018 Reiss-Engelhorn ruling — may still recognise a photographer's interest in museum
reproductions. A credit line costs nothing and removes the argument.

## Sources checked and rejected

Recorded so nobody re-treads it:

- **Smithsonian NMAA — Kobayashi Kiyochika night-railway prints.** These read as CC0 on the object
  page, but the CC0 text there is an unused popover template; the actual rights badge says
  *"Usage conditions apply."* **Not usable.**
- **Angelo Morbelli, *La stazione centrale di Milano*.** The photograph is CC BY-SA (share-alike would
  attach to our page) *and* carries an Italian *beni culturali* notice requiring authorisation for
  commercial reuse. **Blocked.**
- **Absurd Design.** The only free-illustration library whose art actually suits this brand — and its
  free tier is **non-commercial**, despite a lot of secondary sources claiming otherwise. Commercial
  use needs the paid plan.
- **unDraw, Storyset, DrawKit, IRA Design.** Licences are clean; the register is not. They are
  instantly recognisable as seed-stage-SaaS illustration, which the design system bans by name — and
  they are spot-illustration packs that cannot produce a hero environment at all.
